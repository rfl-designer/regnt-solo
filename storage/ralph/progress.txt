## Codebase Patterns

- **Sidebar**: `resources/views/layouts/app/sidebar.blade.php` — grupos: Visão Geral, Planejamento (Work, Hoje, Semana), Acompanhamento, Análise
- **Rotas**: `routes/web.php` — usa `Route::livewire()` com middleware `auth`
- **Features page**: `resources/views/pages/⚡features.blade.php` — SFC Livewire com Kanban
- **Testes Features**: `tests/Feature/FeaturesKanbanTest.php` — usa `route('work')` após renomeação
- **Keyboard shortcuts**: `resources/views/layouts/app.blade.php` — Alpine.js x-on:keydown.window
- **Command Palette**: `resources/views/components/⚡command-palette.blade.php` — referencia shortcuts inline
- **Redirect backward compat**: `Route::redirect('/features', '/work')` adicionado em web.php
- **Table sorting pattern**: `$sortBy`/`$sortDirection` com `#[Url]`, `sort()` method, `sortedFeatures()` computed que ordena collection em memória
